## Page application

Each group has one owning page. Titles and main headings use natural English, including prepositions such as “in” and “from”; they do not have to repeat a search phrase mechanically. The homepage leads with freight forwarders in Slovenia, while logistics companies supports the introduction. The catalog at `/companies` remains a search tool rather than a second homepage targeting the same headline.

Koper and Ljubljana have their own local-office directories. Koper's title, introduction and buyer questions explicitly address finding a freight forwarder for the Port of Koper, while the city-office filter and canonical URL remain unchanged. Ljubljana copy explicitly explains its city/district grouping. The customs page discusses selecting a customs provider and confirming service scope. The sea-freight page covers ocean freight, container shipping, FCL/LCL and onward movement through the Port of Koper. The China guide focuses on the container shipment and quotation components; it does not pretend to show live rates.

The existing `/services/forwarding` page owns the service-planning intent: selecting transport modes, coordinating handovers and agreeing what a complete forwarding proposal includes. Its copy helps buyers define the work, while the homepage remains the broad national provider comparison. A visible homepage buyer question explains the term “logistics forwarder”; it is not repeated mechanically across every page.

Internal discovery links connect these pages using descriptive anchors. Company profiles retain the company-name intent and a single canonical profile URL. A profile is not duplicated under every city or service.

Singular/plural, missing-preposition and close wording variants belong to the same page. “Best freight forwarders in Slovenia” can be addressed through factual buyer guidance; promotional placement is not represented as an independent quality ranking.
